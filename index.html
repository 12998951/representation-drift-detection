Representation Drift Detection in Biometric Systems

Moien Shaik
January 2026

Abstract

Biometric systems increasingly rely on deep neural encoders to extract latent representations from face and voice signals. However, real-world deployments violate the assumption of stationarity, leading to representation drift that can silently degrade system reliability. This project proposes a lightweight, label-free, embedding-level drift detection framework for continuous monitoring of biometric systems under streaming conditions.

Method Overview

Raw biometric inputs are processed by a fixed encoder to produce latent embeddings. A reference window and a current window are maintained over the embedding stream, and drift is detected by monitoring statistical divergence between them.
